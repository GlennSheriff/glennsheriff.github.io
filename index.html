About Me

The Consistent Theme: People

“

"Businesses work better when the people inside them—and the customers they serve—are treated as the starting point."

My career has taken a few turns, but there has been one consistent theme throughout: people. After business school, I spent two years in corporate life, followed by four years working as a therapist. I then spent more than 17 years in corporate leadership—directing marketing strategy, business intelligence, analytics, organizational performance, and integration.

After returning to people-focused work through therapy and professional executive coaching, I transitioned over the past five years into hands-on small business ownership and management across boating, marine retail, hospitality, and e-commerce.

Today, as a small business operator and AI Generalist in training, I focus on practical AI: creating useful, understandable systems that make work easier, sharpen decisions, and help small teams achieve more without ever losing the human side of business.
